Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

We may collect names, organisation and role details, email addresses, phone numbers, billing details, enquiry information and project-related contact information.

3. How we collect information

Information may be collected when you contact us, request a quote, engage us for services, submit a website form or communicate with us by email, phone or online meeting tools.

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals and agreements, deliver services, administer projects, issue invoices and meet legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

5. Disclosure

We may disclose information to service providers, professional advisers, software providers or authorities where reasonably necessary or required by law.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Responsible security reports may be sent to security@dukestg.com.au.

7. Access, correction and complaints

You may request access to or correction of personal information held about you, or raise a privacy concern, by contacting DTG at privacy@dukestg.com.au.

8. Overseas processing

Some technology providers may process or store information outside Australia. We use providers with appropriate security controls.

9. Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will be published on this website.
